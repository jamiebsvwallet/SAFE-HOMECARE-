## 💡 FREE TIPS FOR BEST RESULTS

### Voiceover Tips:
✅ Speak slowly and clearly (if recording yourself)
✅ Use Natural Reader's "David" or "Amy" voice
✅ Record in quiet environment
✅ Use Audacity to remove background noise (free)

### Video Tips:
✅ Keep clips short (3-5 seconds each)
✅ Use high-quality HD footage from Pexels
✅ Match clips to narration timing
✅ Add smooth transitions (built into Canva)

### Music Tips:
✅ Keep volume low (20-30%)
✅ Fade in at start, fade out at end
✅ Choose upbeat corporate style
✅ Ensure music doesn't overpower voiceover

### Text Tips:
✅ Use large, bold fonts
✅ White text with black outline (readable)
✅ Display statistics when mentioned
✅ Keep text on screen 3-5 seconds
